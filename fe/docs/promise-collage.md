# Tatsuo Promise accordion redesign

Reference: user-supplied Margaret's screenshot. Three left-hand accordion panels, active red treatment, charcoal grain, gold dividers, right-hand collage with four product tiles above and four below a central generated butcher preparation photograph. Previous six-icon layout and sauce accents are no longer rendered. The section remains inside the shared Why Choose Us white side animation.

Center photograph generated with the built-in imagegen tool, encoded as WebP: `src/assets/promise-butcher-table-v1.webp`. Product tiles reuse the eight existing catalog illustrations. Generated images illustrate preparation and products, not documentary Tatsuo staff photographs.

Prompt: Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: center photograph in a premium butcher website collage, square composition. Direct overhead editorial photograph of a beautiful dark walnut butcher worktable, two gloved human hands entering from the top carefully arranging freshly sliced raw beef using a long slicing knife, no face, no head. Neat paper-lined tray with a few cuts at upper right, folded ivory butcher paper, restrained sprig of rosemary and a few peppercorns, clean artisan workbench, small stainless tongs, no clutter. Focus on craft, preparation and quality, rich deep burgundy marbling, warm golden light, charcoal shadows, natural realistic materials. The table fills the frame, generous layered texture but confident balanced composition, real professional hospitality campaign photography, not a meat closeup alone. No text, no logo, no watermark, no labels, no beer, no bottles, no blood splatter, no gore, no extra fingers, no impossible tools. Sophisticated warm contrast, 50mm lens look, refined authentic tactile quality. Frame works as square with hands and cutting action centered.
